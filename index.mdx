N2AB Client is a mod client for the [PolyTrack](https://polytrack.skyhost.fun) racing game. It runs alongside the game in your browser or as a standalone Electron desktop application, injecting HUD overlays, telemetry readouts, cinematic tools, and a fully extensible feature panel — all without touching the game's physics or leaderboard systems.

## What is N2AB Client?

N2AB Client is a pre-built, rebranded client bundle that sits on top of PolyTrack. It provides a collapsible side panel with toggleable modules, persistent settings stored in `localStorage`, and a public JavaScript API that lets you register your own modules alongside the built-ins — no build system required.

## Get started in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Play on the hosted client">
    Open [polytrack.skyhost.fun](https://polytrack.skyhost.fun) in your browser — N2AB Client loads automatically with the game.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    Press the toggle key (shown in the top-right corner) to open the N2AB Client side panel. All built-in modules are listed there, ready to enable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your own module">
    Append a `registerFeature` call to `tools/modules.js` and run `node tools/build-n2ab-client.js` to patch it into the bundle. Your module appears in the panel instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  N2AB Client is purely presentational — it adds overlays and readouts but never modifies physics, race timing, or leaderboard submissions.
</Note>
